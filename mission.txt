## Construct a pole-free Virasoro-block recursion at (c=\tfrac12)

Consider the torus one-point Virasoro block

[
\mathcal F_{\Delta}^{(c)}(\Delta_{\rm ext}\mid q)
=================================================

q^{\Delta-c/24}
\sum_{N\ge0} q^N
F_N(c,\Delta,\Delta_{\rm ext}).
]

For generic (c), the Fateev–Litvinov/Zamolodchikov recursion computes this efficiently by summing over simple poles at degenerate dimensions (\Delta_{r,s}(c)).

At rational central charge, different (\Delta_{r,s}) coincide. Individual recursion terms then diverge even though their sum—and the conformal block away from its genuine poles—is finite.

**Problem:** Construct a recursion directly at the Ising central charge

[
c=\frac12
]

that is manifestly finite, without inserting (c=\frac12+\varepsilon) and numerically taking (\varepsilon\to0).

### Concrete deliverable

Derive a formula of the schematic form

[
H_\Delta(q)
===========

1+
\sum_{\lambda}
\sum_{k=1}^{m_\lambda}
\frac{A_{\lambda,k}(q)}
{(\Delta-\lambda)^k},
]

where:

* (\lambda) runs over distinct degenerate dimensions at (c=\tfrac12);
* (m_\lambda) records pole multiplicities created by colliding Kac labels;
* (A_{\lambda,k}) is recursively expressed using shifted blocks and possibly (\Delta)-derivatives;
* every coefficient is finite at (c=\tfrac12).

Then prove that it reproduces the definition from inverse Shapovalov matrices to all orders in (q).

### Why it qualifies

A recent CFT reference explicitly states that finding a recursion at rational (\beta^2)—with torus one-point blocks as the simplest case—is open. [Ribault, *Exactly solvable conformal field theories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17262)

Earlier work established how the spurious poles cancel in examples and characters, but did not produce a general pole-free recursion. [Javerzat–Santachiara–Foda](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.02790) A later thesis computed pole-free torus expressions only through (q^4). [Stocco](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08653)

This matters because Virasoro blocks are basic computational objects in:

* minimal models, Ising/Potts/percolation CFT;
* Liouville theory and minimal strings;
* (AdS_3/CFT_2);
* analytic and numerical 2D conformal bootstrap.

### Likely route

1. Generate exact (F_N) from Shapovalov matrices through (N\approx10)–15.
2. Expand the generic-(c) recursion around (c=\tfrac12+\varepsilon).
3. Group terms by resonant Kac classes before taking (\varepsilon\to0).
4. Recognize the finite confluent limit as higher-order poles and derivatives of shifted blocks.
5. Prove the grouping recursively using the embedding structure of Virasoro null modules.

A strong consistency check is that identity insertion should recover the three Ising characters for internal weights

[
\Delta\in\left{0,\frac12,\frac1{16}\right}.
]

The (c=\tfrac12) theorem alone should be a viable hep-th/math-ph paper. Extending the same construction to all minimal-model charges (c_{p,q}) would turn it into a considerably stronger result.

Compared with the quantum-minimal-surface problem, this one is more recognizably HEP and likely more algebraic/computer-assisted, but also carries greater danger of ugly resonance combinatorics.
