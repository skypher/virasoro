## Design decisions

1. **Virasoro algebra and Verma module (Def 2.1)** — `Virasoro/Algebra.lean`.
   `VirEnv R c` is the quotient (`RingQuot`) of the free algebra on symbols `L n`, `n : ℤ`, by
   the Virasoro relations with central charge `c`; `Verma R c h` is its quotient by the left
   ideal generated by `L n` (`n > 0`) and `L 0 - h`. This is the textbook induced module, so all
   modes act and the commutation relations hold by construction. `R` is any commutative
   `ℚ`-algebra, so the same definition gives complex, polynomial and rational-function
   parameters. What is *not* automatic is the PBW basis and the existence of the vacuum
   functional; these are theorems (see `Stmt_def_matrices`).
2. **Shapovalov and torus matrices (Def 2.2)** — `Virasoro/NormalOrder.lean`.
   `gram c h N` and `torus c h d N` are defined by the normal-ordering procedure of the proof of
   Lemma 2.4 (`actWord`, `gramWord`, `torusWord`), by structural recursion, over any commutative
   `ℚ`-algebra. They are computable, which the Ising computations use
   (`decide +kernel` over `ℚ`). The frozen statement `Stmt_def_matrices` says that they are the
   matrix elements `⟨h|L_Y L_{-Y'}|h⟩` and `⟨h|L_Y V_d(1) L_{-Y'}|h⟩` of the abstract Verma
   module (existence and uniqueness of the vacuum functional and of the primary-field pairing).
   The primary field is encoded by its matrix elements at `z = 1` and the Ward identity
   (`Verma.IsPrimaryPairing`).
3. **Block coefficients (Defs 2.2, 2.3)** — `Virasoro/Block.lean`.
   For a field `𝔽` of characteristic 0 and `c d : 𝔽`, `Frat c d N`, `Hrat c d N : RatFunc 𝔽`
   are rational functions of `Δ`, via Cramer's rule (`adjugate / det`). With `d` indeterminate,
   `𝔽 = Kd = RatFunc ℂ` (`FN`, `HN` in `Statements/Prelude.lean`).
4. **Kac data, residue data, germs** — `Kac.lean`, `Residue.lean`, `Shifted.lean`.
   `kacC`, `kacH`, `genA`, `genP`, `genR` are functions on an arbitrary field, so that complex
   values, rational functions of `b` (`b = RatFunc.X`) and Laurent germs (`b = b⋆ + t`) are
   instances of the same definition. Germs are `LaurentSeries K` with `K = Kd` in statements.
5. **Local expansions** — `Local.lean`: `lcoeff`, `ppCoeff`, `poleOrder`, `PP` through
   `RatFunc.laurent` and the coercion to `LaurentSeries`.
6. **Smith exponents** — `Smith.lean`: recorded as a multiset (`IsSmith G s`).
7. **Generic recursion (Prop 6.5)**: `Stmt_prop_generic_recursion` is the literal statement
   (complex `b` outside a finite set of algebraic numbers). Its consumers (Thm 4.3, Prop 7.7)
   apply it to the germ `b = b⋆ + t`; the form they use is `GenericRecursionUniform`
   (`Statements/Sec6.lean`), which implies the literal statement.

### Re-encoding of Proposition 6.2 (round three)

`ImportedNormalizations` and `Cited_HJSNormalizations` were stated over `ℂ(b)` and the cited
input contained the continuation clause. With coordinates in `ℂ(b)` the points where the
normalized singular vector is regular cannot be cut out by polynomials over `ℚ`, which the
uniform form of Proposition 6.5 needs. The paper's Proposition 6.2 speaks of identities of
rational functions of `b`, and its proof takes the normalized singular vector from a linear
system over `ℚ(b)`. The encoding now mirrors this: `NormalizationIdentities` (the cited
`Cited_HJSNormalizations`) is the existence and uniqueness of the normalized singular vector
over `ℚ(b)` with the identities (6.1), (6.2) there; `Stmt_prop_imported_normalizations` adds the
continuation to complex specializations, which is now proved from the identities.

### How the explicit Ising results at levels three and four are proved

- All explicit data are established over an arbitrary field `K` of characteristic zero
  (vectors `xi2K`, `xi3K`, `chi2K`, `chi4K`, … in Proofs/Ising3, Ising4) and instantiated at
  `K = ℂ` for Propositions 5.11, 5.13, 5.14 and at `K = ℂ(d)` for Theorems 5.12, 5.15. The
  frozen vectors over `ℂ` are definitionally the `K = ℂ` instances.
- Singular vectors: direct commutation with `L_1, …, L_ℓ` in the normal-ordering model
  (`isSingular_of_actSt`), higher modes vanish by level.
- Shapovalov forms of explicit vectors are polynomials in the weight obtained from the
  normal-ordered matrix elements of their words (`gramForm_coords`); crossing scalars and the
  first crossing form are their derivatives (`crossScalar_eq`, `cross1_gramLocal_eq`).
- Determinants: the explicit Shapovalov matrices in the ordered Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt bases
  (`gram2`, `gram3`, `gram4`), expanded and compared by `ring`.
- Smith exponents: determinant valuation equals nullity, then Lemma 5.1
  (`isSmith_of_order_eq_nullity`).
- Residues: Proposition 5.2 in a basis of the kernel (`ppCoeff_Frat_of_basis`) and Lemma 5.9
  (`Verma.singular_factorization`) for the descendants of the level-two singular vector.

## Notes on the proof of Proposition 6.5

- `Cited_VermaHomInjective` is not used: injectivity of the singular embedding on a level
  follows from the nondegeneracy of the crossing form `β_χ G_{N-rs}(c, h+rs)`.
- The passage to all fields of characteristic zero does not go through `ℂ`: the Kac determinant
  is transferred from `ℂ` to `ℚ(b)` (a rational function over `ℚ` vanishing at all positive
  integers is zero) and from there to any field by evaluation on the subring of `ℚ(b)` of
  functions regular at `b` (`kac_det_field`); the normalized singular vector over `ℚ(b)`
  specializes the same way (`normalization_at`).
- The exceptional set for level `N` is the set of roots of the polynomials `m X² + n`
  (`|m|, |n| ≤ 2N`, not both zero) and of the denominators of the coordinates of the normalized
  singular vectors of level at most `N` (`linPolys`, `denomPolys` in Proofs/GenericH).

## Supporting results proved without a paper number

The Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt basis of `Verma R c h` over any commutative `ℚ`-algebra: the vectors
`L_{-Y}|h⟩` over all partitions are linearly independent (`Verma.neg_word_linearIndependent`,
Proofs/PBWIndep, through the polynomial realization `L_{-m} ↦ x_m + ∑ k x_{k+m} ∂_k`) and span
(`Verma.neg_mem_level`, `Verma.iSup_level_eq_top`, Proofs/PBWSpan). Freeness of the Verma module
over `U(Vir_-)` (`Verma.exists_negHom`, Proofs/VermaFree; the Virasoro action on `U(Vir_-)` is
constructed in Proofs/Induced). Existence, uniqueness and symmetry of the Shapovalov form
(`Verma.isShapovalov_shap`, `Verma.shapovalov_unique`, `Verma.IsShapovalov.symm`, Proofs/Shapovalov).
The homomorphism `M(c,h') → M(c,h)` of a singular vector (`Verma.exists_singularHom`). The identity
`∑ η_j p(N-j) = δ_{N,0}` (`eta_partition`). Partial fractions over a field (`isPoleSum_div`,
`IsPoleSum.eq_sum_PP`).

Existence of the local Smith form over `K⟦X⟧` (`exists_isSmith`); the layer dimension formula
(`finrank_layer`); the Fredholm alternative for symmetric matrices (`symm_range_of_orth`);
naturality of `gram`/`torus` under ring homomorphisms (`gram_map`, `torus_map`); nonvanishing of
the Shapovalov determinant as a polynomial in the internal weight (`gramPoly_det_ne_zero`, by
the leading-term analysis in the proof of Lemma 4.1); `A⋆ ∈ ℚ(b⋆²)[d]` (`astar_isPolyOver`);
uniqueness of partial fractions through Laurent expansions (`ppCoeff_sum`).
