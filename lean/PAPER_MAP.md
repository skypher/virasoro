# Paper results and their Lean statements (papers as of 2026-11-26)

Generated by scripts/papermap.py from `paper.aux`, `sphere/paper.aux`, `irreducible/paper.aux`
and the `Paper label:` docstrings.  On 2026-11-25 the two manuscripts were split into three:
`sphere/paper.tex` (the large-weight limit, the old sphere Sections 2-3 and Appendix A),
`paper.tex` (Part I: the torus one-point block, old torus Sections 2-8 reordered; Part II: the
old sphere Sections 4-6, labels that clash with Part I carry the suffix `-sph`) and
`irreducible/paper.tex` (the old torus Sections 9-14; its Section 2 quotes results of
`paper.tex`, listed here with the same Lean statements).  The frozen statement files
(`Virasoro/Statements/`, `Virasoro/Sphere/Statements/`) keep the numbering and line references
of 70bac53; this table gives the current numbers.  Where a paper restated a result after
70bac53, the Lean statement in the paper's current form has the suffix `_paper` (2026-11-24,
Rev20261124.lean), `_rev25` (2026-11-25, Rev20261125.lean) or `_rev26` (2026-11-26,
Rev20261126.lean, traces paper: BGG weight as a Gamma quotient) or is new, and is proved from
the earlier form.  Every listed theorem depends only on [propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound] (`../runs/lean2/checkMAP.log`); cited inputs (`Cited_*`) are hypotheses, as in the
papers.

Notation (2026-11-27): `paper.tex` renamed some symbols; no statement, definition or number
changed, and the Lean docstrings keep the old names.  Section 7.2: the label weight `w_g = h_g(b⋆)` is
now `h⋆_g` and the colliding group `G^{(M)}_μ` is `𝒢^{(M)}_μ`; Lemma 7.5: the pole order `π_g` is `e_g`;
Section 8.1: the signed momenta `S_ν` are `m̃_ν`, the chain weights `w_γ` are `ρ_γ`, `ℓ(γ)` is `ρ̂_γ`,
and the layer differences `Δ` of Lemmas 8.3 to 8.7 and 8.10 are `D(ν,ν')`; Section 8.2: `T_k` is `m̃_k`, `M_k`
is `m_k`, `Δ_m = μ_{i+m} - μ_i` in Theorem 8.11(iii) is `ℓ_m`, and `τ₀` is `α₀`; Proposition 9.3,
Case 3: the ratio `T_k` is `X_k`.  Later the same day: in Proposition 9.1 the variable `ν = b²` is `t`
(and `ν⋆` is `t⋆`), and `ϱ`, `ϱ_g` are `ϑ`, `ϑ_g` (also in the Section 7 example, Proposition 9.3 and
Section 10); in Proposition 9.2 the coefficients `c^{(ν)}_{i,j}(M)` are `ξ^{(ν)}_{i,j}(M)` and the
degree `D` in the proof of (iv) is `n`; in the proof of Theorem 13.4 the exponent `κ` is `E`.
