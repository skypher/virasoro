## Content fit

The cited work on the same recursions appeared in JHEP (Poghossian 2009,
Hadasz–Jaskólski–Suchanek 2010, Javerzat–Santachiara–Foda 2018, Cho–Collier–Yin
2019, Beşken–Datta–Kraus 2020), Commun. Math. Phys. (Zamolodchikov 1984,
Astashkevich 1997), SciPost Phys. (Ribault 2019), Nucl. Phys. B (BPZ 1984,
Felder 1989, Di Francesco–Saleur–Zuber 1987) and J. Phys. A (Gaberdiel–Lang 2009).
J. Phys. A lists "conformal field theories" and "symmetry and representation theory"
in its scope and asks that mathematical papers be "clearly motivated by actual or
potential application to physical phenomena"; each introduction opens with the
physics use (bootstrap, modular covariance, minimal-model traces).
The JHEP papers the torus recursion comes from are derivations with numerics; these
papers state and prove theorems, closer to CMP, LMP and J. Phys. A.

## Presentation against the venues

- Length: J. Phys. A papers are "usually approximately 10000 words (18 journal
  pages)". The large-weight paper (11,002 words) fits; the traces paper (17,115) and
  the colliding-poles paper (27,368, two parts in one paper) are well above. SIGMA has
  no limit. Springer limits not read.
- Layout: abstract, introduction with the main results and the literature, setup,
  proofs, examples, scope section, back matter. This is the usual math-phys order.
  Abstracts are 169, 87 and 138 words.
- AI disclosure: the papers carry a two-sentence unnumbered section with no tool
  names. IOP wants it in the Acknowledgements and naming the tool and how outputs
  were verified; AIP wants it in Methods with name, version and manufacturer;
  Springer wants Methods plus an end declaration with tool versions, dates and
  prompts. The short form has to be expanded to the chosen journal's form at
  submission (the global rule allows more detail when the journal requires it).
- Code availability: present in all three; github.com/skypher/virasoro is public
  (checked 2026-11-26 with `gh repo view`).
- Footnotes that only gave equation numbers of cited papers were moved inline on
  2026-11-26; citation keys are bracketed author-year, which journals restyle.
- Custom page style (article class, 5.5in text, own running heads): fine for arXiv
  and submission; J. Phys. A and SIGMA use their own class after acceptance.
