## arXiv's rule (policy updated 2026-01-21)

- An endorsement is needed for a first submission to a category.
- The exception is automatic endorsement. It needs both an institutional email on the arXiv account and a previously accepted paper by the same author in that endorsement domain.
- hep-th is its own endorsement domain.
- An endorser must be a registered author with a certain number of papers in the domain, counting only papers submitted between 3 months and 5 years ago. arXiv does not publish the number.
- The definitive check is the link "Which authors of this paper are endorsers?" on an abstract page. It needs an arXiv login, so it was not checked here.
- To get the link: start a submission with hep-th as the primary category. arXiv emails an endorsement link to forward.
- An endorser's email address is under "Submission history" on their abstract pages. arXiv asks authors to contact one person at a time.
