# Prior Work on Irreducible Minimal-Model Torus One-Point Traces (`T_IRR`)

Targeted literature audit before formulating novelty claims or writing `irreducible/paper.tex`. All citations below were verified directly against the local copies in `/tmp/virasoro_refs/` or the companion repository files.

## 1. Verma-module structure and minimal-model characters

- **Rocha-Caridi (1985)**, *Vacuum vector representations of the Virasoro algebra* (in *Vertex Operators in Mathematics and Physics*, MSRI Publ. 3, pp. 451--473): derives the irreducible minimal-model character ($d = 0$ torus trace)
  $$\operatorname{Tr}_{L(c_\star, h_{r,s})}(q^{L_0 - h_{r,s}}) = \frac{1}{\phi(q)}\sum_{j \in \mathbb{Z}} \bigl(q^{\ell_{2j}} - q^{\ell_{2j+1}}\bigr)$$
  from the braided embedding structure of Feigin--Fuchs. Applies only to the identity insertion $V_0(1) = 1$ ($d = 0$), where the Verma trace is simply $\phi(q)^{-1}$ independent of the weight.
- **Feigin--Fuchs (1984, 1990)** and **Astashkevich (1997)** (`arXiv:hep-th/9511032`, verified pp. 5, 11--16):
  - Theorem 3.1 (p. 5): uniqueness of a singular vector at each level and polynomiality of its PBW coefficients in $(h, c)$ when normalized with coefficient $1$ on $L_{-1}^n$.
  - Theorem A(a), (b)(iii), (c) (pp. 12--13): in Case $\mathrm{III}_-$ (which includes every interior minimal-model Kac weight $\lambda = h_{r,s}(b_\star)$ and every weight in its BGG orbit), all submodules of $M(c_\star, \mu)$ are generated by singular vectors, the embedding diagram is braided, and every Verma submodule generated by a singular vector again belongs to Case $\mathrm{III}_-$.
  - Theorem B(e) (pp. 14--15): the Jantzen filtration along any curve transverse to the Kac curves coincides with the braided layer filtration $J^k = W^+_k + W^-_k$.
  - Theorem C(e) (p. 16): the Jantzen filtration along the first Kac curve $F(k_1, l_1)$ has $M^{(1)} = M(\nu_1) + M(\nu_2)$ and $M^{(i)} = M(\nu_1)$ for all $i \ge 2$.
- **Iohara--Koga (2011)**, *Representation Theory of the Virasoro Algebra* (Springer): Propositions 5.1, 5.2, Corollary 5.2 (regularity and normalization of Kac singular vectors), Proposition 5.4 and Figure 5.4 (braided embedding diagram in Class $R^+$, Case $1+$), and Theorem 6.3 (Jantzen filtration).

## 2. Free-field / BRST torus one-point functions

- **Felder (1989)** (*Nucl. Phys. B* 317, 215) and **Felder--Silvotti (1989)** (*Phys. Lett. B* 231, 411; *Comm. Math. Phys.* 123, 1): realize $L(c_\star, \lambda)$ as the cohomology of a complex of Feigin--Fuchs Fock modules and write minimal-model torus one-point functions as alternating sums of traces of screened vertex operators over Fock spaces (requiring multi-dimensional screening contour integrals on the torus). They do not express $\operatorname{Tr}_{L(c_\star,\lambda)}(q^{L_0-\lambda} V_d(1))$ in terms of Verma-module torus one-point blocks or the Poghossian--HJS recursion.

## 3. Generic torus one-point recursion and rational-charge limits

- **Poghossian (2009)** (`arXiv:0909.3412`) and **Hadasz--Jaskolski--Suchanek (2010)** (`arXiv:0911.2353`, §3, eqs. (7)--(9), verified from source): derive the generic-$b$ residue recursion for the eta-reduced Verma torus one-point block $H_N(c(b), \Delta, d)$, with residues $R_{R,S}(b, d) = A_{R,S}(b)\,\tau_{R,S}(b, d)$ and fusion polynomials $P_{R,S}(b; \Delta_1, \Delta_2)$ (also in **Zamolodchikov 2004**, `hep-th/0312279`, §5 eqs. (5.9)--(5.12), §§6--7 eqs. (6.1), (7.5)).
- **Javerzat--Santachiara--Foda (2018)** (`arXiv:1806.02790`, §5, pp. 18--25, verified from source): study how to recover minimal-model characters ($d = 0$ only) from the generic torus one-point recursion via a simultaneous three-parameter limit $\alpha_{\mathrm{ext}} = 2\varepsilon$, $\alpha_{\mathrm{int}} = \varepsilon$, $b = \sqrt{-(p'/p)(1 + \varepsilon'')}$ with $\varepsilon'' = 4\varepsilon/\sqrt{-pp'}$. They derive explicit rational chain weights $C_{N(K)}$ and check numerically up to $N \sim 300$ that $C_{N(K)} = (-1)^{K+1}$, leaving both an all-order proof and the case of nonzero primary insertions $d \ne 0$ open.
- **Ribault (2019)** (`arXiv:1809.03722`, §3) and **Ribault (2026)** (`arXiv:2411.17262v4`, §4.3.3, verified from source): state a recursion for Virasoro blocks at rational $b^2$ as an open problem, noting that torus one-point blocks are its simplest case and citing JSF (2018) on minimal-model characters.
- **Companion torus paper (`paper.tex`, commit `299e8a0`, reviewer PASS)**: proves (i) all-order exact algebraic confluence at fixed $b_\star$ for the *Verma-module* torus one-point block (Theorem 4.3), and (ii) exact pole order $j_N(\lambda)$ and top-layer formula at minimal-model interior weights for *indeterminate* $d$ (Theorems 5.8, 5.9, Corollary 5.10). Does not treat the irreducible quotient traces $\operatorname{Tr}_{L(c_\star,\lambda)}(q^{L_0-\lambda} V_d(1))$ or pole cancellation at special fusion-allowed $d$.

## 4. What is genuinely new in our `T_IRR` results

None of the above papers prove:
1. The exact iff characterization: $\rho(c_\star, \lambda, d)$ descends to $L(c_\star, \lambda)$ $\iff$ $d \in \mathcal{F}_{p,p'}(r,s)$ $\iff$ the Verma block $F_N(c_\star, \Delta, d)$ is regular at $\Delta = \lambda$ for all $N \ge 0$ (all $j_N(\lambda)$ Jantzen poles cancel identically).
2. The exact two-variable fusion-polynomial derivative formula for the first-layer Jantzen vertex ratio $c_{R,S}(t; 2k+1, 2m+1)$ and its exact factorization as a ratio of Gamma potentials $\Psi_{2k+1, 2m+1}(h_{R,-S})/\Psi_{2k+1, 2m+1}(h_{R,S})$.
3. The two-layer $t$-Jantzen identity on the generic degenerate quotient $V_{R,S}(b_\star + t)$ and the resulting generalized Rocha--Caridi / BGG alternating-sum identity expressing $\operatorname{Tr}_{L(c_\star, \lambda)}(q^{L_0-\lambda} V_d(1))$ as an exact finite linear combination (at each $q^N$) of regular Verma torus one-point blocks at the BGG orbit weights.
4. Exact all-order confluence for irreducible minimal-model torus one-point traces from the generic Poghossian--HJS recursion with no numerical $\varepsilon$-limit, including the completely collision-free specialization at $d = h_{1,2m+1}(b_\star)$ and $d = h_{2k+1,1}(b_\star)$.
